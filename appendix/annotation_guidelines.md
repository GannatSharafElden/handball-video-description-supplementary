# Handball-specific annotation rubric (paper-based summary)

**Provenance:** This is a structured summary of the annotation procedure reported in §3.2 of the manuscript, **not an exact transcript of the instructions presented to the human annotator**.

## Purpose

For each 16-second handball clip, write a concise, visually grounded natural-language description of the principal event and its temporal development. Describe only what can be established from the footage.

## Topics to consider

1. **Player positioning:** when visible, identify roles such as goalkeeper, wing, pivot, left/right back and center back.
2. **Core actions:** passes, shots, defensive actions, blocks, goalkeeper saves, counterattacks, penalties and set attacks.
3. **Officiating cues:** mention referees, referee signals or decisions only when supported by a visible signal or evident stoppage.
4. **Visual limitations:** avoid unsupported statements about distant jersey numbers, ball possession, gestures or other obscured features.
5. **Temporal development:** summarize the order of the main visible actions, rather than simply listing objects.
6. **Context:** include team colours, ball movement, team interactions and court context when clearly discernible.

## Evidence policy

- Do not invent player identities, possession, outcomes or referee decisions that are not adequately visible.
- If the ball is obscured, do not assign possession unless movement and visual context support that interpretation.
- Avoid frame-by-frame narration when a concise clip-level summary conveys the main event.

## Example reported in the manuscript

> Player #4 from the green team shoots a penalty, but the goalkeeper saves it.
